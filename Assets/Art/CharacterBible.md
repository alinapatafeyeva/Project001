# Mofu Character Bible

## Overview

Mofu is the main character species of the game.
Cute, fluffy, always hungry, and expressive.

The design should be simple, highly readable on mobile devices, and recognizable even at small sizes.

---

## Shape

- Almost circular body.
- Slightly wider at the bottom.
- Tiny feet.
- No visible arms.
- Large fluffy silhouette.

---

## Fur

- Medium-length fluffy fur.
- Soft rounded outline.
- No sharp spikes.
- Simple shading.

---

## Face

- Two large round eyes.
- Thick expressive eyebrows.
- Small mouth.
- Two tiny fangs.

---

## Personality

- Cute.
- Always a little hungry.
- Never scary.
- Funny when angry.
- Extremely happy after eating.

---

## Readability

- Must be recognizable at 64×64 px.
- Avoid tiny details.
- Strong silhouette.
- Facial expression should be readable instantly.

---

## States

- Hungry
- Very Hungry
- Satisfied

---

## Animation Style

Idle:
- Gentle breathing.
- Small body bounce.
- Occasional blink.

Hungry:
- Faster breathing.
- Eyebrows lowered.

Satisfied:
- Relaxed body.
- Happy smile.
- Slow blinking.

---

## Accessories

Supported:

- Hats
- Glasses
- Scarves
- Seasonal costumes
- Holiday accessories

Accessories must never hide the face.
